# Promo Playbook — Concierge of Airbnbs (efficient, low-budget, high-odd)

**The one-line identity** (say this everywhere):

> "There's a doorman for other people's Airbnbs — bookings, the money, and the paperwork when the keys change hands."

## A. The style kit (the "unheard-of" look)

- **The Doorman page** = the flagship promo (night facade, one pool of brass light,
  intercom buzzboard). It's interactive, prints like a valet stub, and every CTA is a
  one-click prefilled email. Stationery, cards, and post art all live in this world:
  - **Hosted site:** `site/index.html` (sales), `site/doorman.html` (promo/stunt),
    `site/forms-pack.html` (lead magnet + paperwork service).
  - Motifs to reuse: brass 🔔, tear-along-the-dotted-line, "VACANT ✕" door hanger,
    desk-log honesty ("stays handled: 0"), the NOT-legible-but-distinct kerning.
- **No algorithms energy:** everything we post leads with _handled_, _butler_,
  _lettered by hand_ — never "book now", "best price", "instantly".

## B. The launch engine (starts at $0)

1. **Host it (one command once, 10 min):** Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages →
   `concierge-of-airbnbs/site/` → set `CONTACT_EMAIL`. Then swap that email into
   `index.html` + `doorman.html` and re-push.
2. **First-five guarantee** (also on the doorman desk log): first 5 stays get the
   concierge fee free — converts "interesting" into "call now".
3. **Lead magnet = the Key Hand-Off Pack:** `forms-pack.html` (Change of Ownership +
   updated Addendum, printable/notarizable). Anyone who clicks gets the email gate →
   that address becomes the concierge's first phone book.

## C. The three ready-to-fire posts (copy inside)

1. **X / IG (never seen before angle):**
   > "Airbnb sold. Next guest already booked. Nobody noticed — because the doorman was
   > on duty. Concierge of Airbnbs: bookings, the money, and the ownership-change
   > paperwork, handled. 🎩 by Mark J Russell"
   > Art: the VACANT ✕ hanger flipping to _occupied_.
2. **Local / FB groups (owners, no adspend):**
   > "Selling your Airbnb? Your _calendar_ isn't for sale. We close the loop a transfer
   > usually breaks — Change of Ownership + updated addendum, notarized, and your
   > booked guests never feel it. Free printable set in the link."
3. **Email / mailer (short list):**
   > "Dear host — when your keys change hands, most agencies drop at least one booking.
   > Ours is the one that doesn't. Reply and we'll put your listing on the board —
   > first five stays, concierge fee on us."

## D. Handshake partners (no money, big reach)

- **Title/escrow offices & real-estate attorneys** — they meet a property CHANGING OWNERS
  weekly. Give them the free pack; owners walk out with us.
- **Home inspectors + tax preparers** — same trigger (property transfer, filing season).
- **Cleaning partners / property managers** — they know every rented listing that
  changes hands; referral = their jobs get booked through us + a referral finder's fee.
- **Travel nurses / locum medics, relocation firms** — a B2B renter who needs a
  _reliable, non-algorithm_ weekly stay. One intro = recurring bookings.

## E. Mechanics that compound

- **Refer a host → your own stays handled fee-free for a month.** Hosts bring hosts;
  renters bring renters (word-of-mouth midwestern velocity).
- **The catches are the campaign:** "stays handled: 0 — yours could be the first."
  Early proof-of-work honesty is the scarcity lever.
- **Every CTA is a tracked mailto** — subject lines already say which door they came in
  (buzzboard / index / forms). Tally the subjects after week 1: that's your ad budget.

## F. Order of operations (what to post, in what order) — user-run

1. Host site → set email → link shareable.
2. Post 1 (X/IG) into the influencer channel wiring already on this machine.
3. eMail post 3 to the first 20 warm contacts (no list? then 2 = first 2 hosts).
4. Print 10 one-sheets (forms-pack → print CSS) → hand to 3 title/escrow + 3 agent offices.
5. Freebie gate live on the doorman page → start the roster ledger when the first email lands.
