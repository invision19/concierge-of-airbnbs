# NAICS Codes — Concierge of Airbnbs (Mark J Russell LLC)

Which NAICS code to put on the EIN application, business license, DBA filing, and tax
returns. The LLC has three activity lines; one is primary, the others are secondary/alternate.

## The three lines — DECIDED

| Activity                             | What it is                                                                                                    | Code       | Official title                                        |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Airbnb/rental property (PRIMARY)** | Operating short-term rental properties and the Airbnb-style rental line                                       | **531110** | Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings        |
| Booking concierge (secondary)        | Middleman between renters and available Airbnb owners: takes bookings, facilitates payment, coordinates stays | 561599     | All Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services |
| Forms & documents service            | Drafting ownership-change forms, addenda, and transaction docs for clients                                    | 561410     | Document Preparation Services                         |

## Decision (approved 2026-09-26)

- **Primary NAICS = `531110` on the DBA, business license, and EIN** — this matches the
  user's directive that the venture be coded as an **airbnb rental** NAICS, and it is the
  code Airbnb-style hosts/operators commonly file under.
- **Secondary = `561599`** (booking/concierge middleman) — attach to the same filings where
  the form allows multiple codes; it covers the coordinate-bookings-and-transactions activity.
- **Document drafting revenue = `561410`** (or fold under 531110/561599 if incidental).

> Tip: rent and also book — keep all three, primary = 531110 per above. The IRS and lenders
> mainly want the **primary** matching where most receipts come from; the mechanism that
> collects the money (the concierge) still reports under the primary unless it dominates.

## What this means for the DBA filing

On the DBA form, the **business activity description** should say:

> "Airbnb-style short-term vacation rental operations and vacation-rental concierge and
> booking agency: coordinating short-term rental bookings and transactions between renters
> and Airbnb owners; document preparation for rental ownership changes and addenda."

Then select primary NAICS `531110` per above.
