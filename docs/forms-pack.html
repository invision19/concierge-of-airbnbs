2 · Existing bookings

Bookings confirmed as of the Effective Date are honored on their existing terms. Security deposits held for such bookings transfer to Transferee, who shall refund or apply them per Airbnb platform rules. Where Transferee cannot fulfill a booking, the Concierge rebooks or cancels per Airbnb policy in good faith.

3 · Payout flows

The Concierge continues to process guest payments and disburse each stay’s proceeds to Transferee from the Effective Date forward. Proceeds for stays completed before the Effective Date settle with Transferor outside the concierge flow.

Transferor warrants the right to transfer the operations, no undisclosed claims or liens blocking the transfer, and deposits/prepaid amounts as disclosed. Transferee acknowledges it has inspected the Property (or shall before its first managed stay) and accepts operations "as available."

5 · Concierge consent & continuity

6 · Indemnity
