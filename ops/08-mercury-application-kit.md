# Ops 08 — Mercury Application Kit (business checking)

Companion to ops/07. Everything an application to **Mercury** will ask for, pre-answered for
Mark J Russell LLC — so the actual application is a ~10 minute owner-in-browser sit-down, not a
puzzle. Mercury is the recommended provider (2026: free checking/savings, free ACH + wires, no
minimums, fintech with partner banks Choice Financial / Column N.A., FDIC). Alternate providers
if Mercury declines: Bluevine, then Novo, then Lili (all no-ChexSystems; keep this kit, swap the
company-verification screens — the answers are the same).

## Preconditions — do these FIRST (each is quick)

1. **LLC formed.** Mark J Russell LLC must exist as a filed entity (state filing completed) with
   your Articles of Organization / formation certificate in hand. Mercury will not open on a
   not-yet-formed entity. (Sole-prop route exists but negates the liability structure — avoid.)
2. **EIN issued.** IRS EIN (free, instant at irs.gov). Kept with the formation docs. Mercury asks
   for the EIN and entity formation date.
3. **Business address** you can verify (street address; Mercury is typically wary of PO boxes).
4. **Government ID** for the owner (passport or driver's license) — Mercury identity-checks the
   beneficial owner.

## How the flow actually runs

1. Owner opens https://mercury.com and starts the application.
2. Entity + EIN screens → then the **beneficial-owner identity** screen. This is the step that CANNOT
   be done by an assistant: Mercury will verify the owner's identity (government ID; sometimes a
   live/proof-of-life check). Complete it in the owner's own browser, on their own phone where asked.
3. Upload formation certificate + EIN letter if prompted.
4. Review, sign electronic agreements, done — typically minutes-to-a-day.

## Honest notes

- "No ChexSystems" means no ChexSystems _record_ gates the account. Mercury still runs identity +
  compliance checks (possibly Early Warning Services) — that's a verification, not a personal-credit
  decision. A personal-credit history or fraud-case file is not something to disclose unprompted and
  is not what Mercury underwrites a checking account on.
- Personal-credit products (business cards, lines of credit) are a SEPARATE decision and can pull
  personal FICO — defer those (ops/07 "Account criteria").
- Application requires the entity to already exist. If formation is still pending, that is the real
  blocker, not the bank.
- The owner's identity step is by design (BSA/KYC). Nobody can "apply for you" past that screen —
  plan around it: paperwork applies, owner confirms identity, then it's open.
