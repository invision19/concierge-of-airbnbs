## Onboarding checklist

1. ☐ Owner signs Agreement + current Addendum (Form 03).
2. ☐ Payout account verified (micro-deposit or linked-account check).
3. ☐ Listing verified live (photos, rules, availability exported).
4. ☐ Cleaning/emergency contacts confirmed in writing.
5. ☐ Owner added to the available roster with fee shown on every confirm.

## Change of ownership (use Form 02)

- [ ] New owner completes a fresh onboard record; payouts re-route from Effective Date.
- [ ] Refund/deposit liability checklist run (what deposits exist, who holds them).
- [ ] Form 02 notarized where state-required; copy to both owners + the ledger.
