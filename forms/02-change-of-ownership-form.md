# Form 02 — Change of Ownership (Vacation Rental / Airbnb Operations)

**Set for:** transferring a vacation rental's hosting & booking operations between owners.
Used when a property (or its rental operation) changes hands and the incoming owner takes
over the listing, bookings, deposits, and concierge relationship.

> Template for document-preparation service. Not legal advice. Have a licensed professional
> review before first use. Print on letterhead; fill blanks; sign in front of a notary where
> indicated. Publisher: **Mark J Russell LLC, d/b/a Concierge of Airbnbs.**

### 1. Transfer of operations

On the Effective Date of ________****, 20****, Transferor assigns and transfers to Transferee
all of Transferor's rights, duties, and interests in the rental operation for the Property,
including without limitation: existing and future bookings; the hosting authority for the
listing; the Concierge services relationship; and the right to receive all booking proceeds
from the Effective Date forward.

### 2. Existing bookings

- Bookings already confirmed as of the Effective Date shall be honored on their existing
  terms. Cleaning/check-in/check-out arrangements continue unchanged unless replaced in writing.
- Security deposits held for such bookings transfer to Transferee, who shall refund or
  apply them per Airbnb platform rules.
- Where a booking cannot be fulfilled by Transferee, Transferee shall use the Concierge to
  rebook or cancel per Airbnb policy, and Transferor shall reasonably cooperate.

### 3. Payout flows (transaction middleman)

The Concierge continues to process guest payments and shall disburse each stay's proceeds to
**Transferee** (as the new owner) from the Effective Date forward, per the Agreement plus the
current Addendum. Transferor's right to proceeds ends on the Effective Date except for stays
completed before it, which the parties settle between themselves outside the concierge flow.

### 4. Representations and warranties

- **Transferor** warrants it has the right to transfer the operations, there are no undisclosed
  claims or liens that block the transfer, and all deposit/prepaid amounts are as disclosed.
- **Transferee** acknowledges it has inspected the Property (or shall do so before its first
  managed stay) and accepts the operations "as available."

### 5. Concierge consent & continuity

The Concierge consents to this transfer and shall continue to provide booking and
transaction services under the existing Agreement and updated Addendum on the same terms.

### 6. Indemnity

Transferor and Transferee each indemnify the Concierge against claims arising from acts or
omissions on their own side (pre- vs post-dating the Effective Date) except the Concierge's
own negligence or willful misconduct.

### 7. Effective Date & priority

This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and, along with the updated Addendum,
supersedes any contrary prior arrangement between the parties regarding the transfer.
