### 3. Concierge role (middleman)

The Concierge coordinates bookings and processes transactions between the Owner and Renters:

- **Booking:** receives the renter's intended stay, confirms availability, and places the
  reservation with the Owner's listing.
- **Payment:** collects the stay amount from the Renter (card/transfer), holds it, and
  disburses to the **Owner** on the schedule below.
- The Concierge is a facilitator — it is not the Renter's or Owner's agent for anything
  outside booking and transaction settlement and assumes no platform or property liability
  beyond its own negligence.

### 6. Cancellation & rebooking (updated terms)

- Renter cancellation: per this policy — full refund ≥ 7 days before check-in; **50%** 3–7 days
  before; no refund < 3 days before, unless rebooked by Concierge at no loss (then a full
  credit/refund minus any Concierge fee already paid).
- Owner-initiated cancellation: Owner refunds the Renter in full and pays any Concierge fee
  already originated; Concierge rebooks comparable dates if available.
- Force majeure (weather, platform outage, emergency): handled per Airbnb policy with good-faith
  remediating.

### 7. Deposits, damages, disputes

- Security/hold amounts follow Airbnb rules. Damage claims: Owner notifies the Concierge
  within 48h of check-out with photos; Concierge manages the resolution between parties.
- Disputes: parties attempt good-faith resolution via the Concierge first; unresolved
  disputes proceed per the original Agreement's governing law (state: ______________).

### 8. Taxes & bookkeeping

Owner is responsible for its own lodging/income taxes; Concierge provides a per-booking
transaction record (date, guest, amount, fee) for the Owner's books upon request.
